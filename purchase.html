Plant Parent Guide
Simple Care Tips

Keep your green friends thriving with these fundamental principles every plant parent should know.

01
☀️ Light is Everything
Match your plant to your light conditions. Most indoor plants prefer bright, indirect light near east or north-facing windows. Avoid harsh afternoon sun on delicate foliage.
02
💧 Water Wisely
Overwatering kills more plants than underwatering. Always check the soil moisture before watering — the top inch should be dry. Always use pots with drainage holes.
03
🪴 Pick the Right Pot
Choose pots 1–2 inches larger than the root ball. Terracotta breathes well for moisture-sensitive plants. Glazed ceramic retains water better for tropical varieties.
04
🌱 Feed During Growth
Fertilise monthly during spring and summer with a balanced liquid fertilizer. Reduce or stop feeding in autumn and winter when plant growth naturally slows down.
05
✂️ Prune Regularly
Remove yellow or dead leaves promptly to direct energy to healthy growth. Pruning also encourages bushier, fuller plants — don't be afraid to cut back.
06
🌡️ Mind the Temperature
Most indoor plants thrive between 18–28°C. Keep them away from cold drafts, air conditioner vents, and sudden temperature swings that stress the roots.
